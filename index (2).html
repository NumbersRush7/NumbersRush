Numbers Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Numbers Rush ("the app") handles information. Contact: contact.numbersrush@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

Numbers Rush does not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information ourselves. We do not run our own servers for the app.

Game data stored on your device. Your progress (coins, XP, streak, lives, settings and best scores) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your region, you will be asked for consent through Google's consent form. You can change or withdraw your choice and reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party receiving data through the app is Google (AdMob), as described above.

4. Children

Numbers Rush is a mental-arithmetic game with no violence, no inappropriate content and no real-money purchases. It is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through ads, contact us and we will help.

5. Your choices and rights

You can delete all locally stored game data by uninstalling the app. You can manage ad personalisation in your device settings. If you have questions or requests about your data, email us at the address above.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
